What is Genesis?
Genesis creates a universe made from a sequence of geometric shapes. You choose how that universe is generated, explore it, and then — if you dare — ENGAGE WARP and travel beyond its primordial boundary into an infinite continuation.

CREATE · Shape & Colour

  • Shape — choose Triangle, Square, Pentagon, Hexagon, Octagon, Circle or Line.
  • Start / End — choose the beginning and ending colours.
  • Palette Center — controls where the colour transition is visually centred.

CREATE · Geometry

  • Initial Scale — size of the first shape.
  • Scale Factor — controls how quickly each successive shape grows.
  • Rotation — progressively rotates successive shapes.
  • Line Width — controls the thickness of the geometry.
  • Intensity — changes the visual strength of the colours without changing the geometry.

GENERATE · How it behaves

  • Mode — Static, Forward, Repeat or Breath.
  • Delay — controls the time between generated shapes in the animated modes.
  • GENERATE — creates the universe using your current settings.

EXPLORE · Observer

NEAR ↔ FAR
Use the Observer slider to move your view through the generated universe. This is the best way to stop and inspect the geometry before travelling.

TRAVEL · GO vs ENGAGE

  • GO — travel normally toward the deepest, primordial part of the generated universe. STOP lets you stop and inspect it.
  • ENGAGE — enter WARP. The primordial point becomes a landmark rather than a final destination, and the journey continues into an infinite mathematical sequence.
WARP EXPERIENCE
When WARP begins, the Bridge disappears so the universe fills the screen. Press G or the floating G button to bring the Bridge back. Press G again to hide it.

Genesis colors in WARP is optional. OFF gives a clean monochrome WARP; ON alternates white with the selected arrival colour.

RESET

RESET restores the default Genesis configuration and generates it again.
