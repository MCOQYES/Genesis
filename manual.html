What is Genesis 0.8?
Genesis 0.8 is about the experience of entering, inhabiting and exploring Genesis. It adds a welcome sequence, YOUR UNIVERSE backgrounds, Natural Zoom and SURPRISE ME while preserving the generative engine, your own shapes, Superposition and WARP.

USE ME BABY · WELCOME

Starting Génesis… appears briefly while the experience begins. Genesis then generates without requiring setup. When the generation completes, Explore & Create your own Genesis → invites you to open the controls.

The welcome is deliberately quiet: no tutorial, no forced setup and no preliminary explanation. Genesis should be seen before it is explained.

YOUR UNIVERSE · BACKGROUND

  • STARS — restores the bundled star field.
  • COLOR — uses the selected colour as the background.
  • IMAGE — choose a local image file for the background.
The background is a stage. It does not participate in Genesis geometry, generation calculations or SVG structure. Images fill the viewport independently in X and Y; Genesis remains unchanged.

NATURAL ZOOM

  • Mouse wheel / trackpad — zoom continuously through the completed universe.
  • Cursor focus — the point under the cursor becomes the centre of the zoom, so you can inspect a particular structure naturally.
  • Touch — pinch to zoom around the midpoint between your fingers.
  • Observer — the existing NEAR ↔ FAR control remains available and can reset the focus to the centre.

Natural Zoom changes the view, not the generated universe. WARP remains its own travelling experience.

SURPRISE ME

SURPRISE ME deliberately explores Genesis rather than trying to guarantee a beautiful result. It uses controlled families and ranges so the randomness remains inside the visual language of Genesis.
  • Shapes and colour modes are varied.
  • Growth factors favour controlled, relatively small steps.
  • Line widths stay within a restrained range.
  • Strokes vary between Solid, Dashed and Dotted, including proportional behaviour where appropriate.
  • Rotation is controlled rather than arbitrary. Simple directional primitives such as Line, X, Cross, Parallel and Radial benefit from rotation more often; structured shapes can remain still more frequently.
  • The most recent families are avoided where practical, reducing immediate repetition.
  • Your persisted Custom Primitive is not replaced by SURPRISE ME.

STROKES · 0.7.1

Stroke controls only the way generated geometry is rendered. It does not modify the Primitive or the generation geometry.
  • Solid — continuous line.
  • Dashed — repeated dash pattern.
  • Dotted — repeated dot pattern.
  • Proportional — available for Dashed and Dotted. When enabled, the pattern grows with the Genesis generation rather than remaining fixed.

COLORS · 0.7.2

  • Gradient — continuous progression from Start to End.
  • Alternate — consecutive generations alternate between Start and End.
  • Three Colors — uses Start, Middle and End with a narrow Middle band centred on Palette Center.
  • Monochrome — one selected colour, optionally progressing from white toward it.
  • Spectrum — continuous traversal of the visible colour spectrum through the generated universe.
  • Palette Center — controls the visual centre of Gradient and Three Colors.

CREATE · YOUR OWN SHAPES

Genesis retains the 0.6 Primitive workflow: canonical space [-1,+1] × [-1,+1], LINE and ARC segments, precision targeting, MOVE, rulers and live coordinates. ARC is defined by three clicks: CENTER → START → END. Custom Primitives persist locally and can be included in exported configurations.

SUPERPOSITION

ACCUMULATE lets a new generation coexist with the current universe. LAYERS shows accumulated generations. UNDO removes the most recent layer. CLEAR removes the accumulated universe without changing current parameter values.

PERSISTENCE · CONFIG

EXPORT CONFIG saves the current Genesis configuration. IMPORT CONFIG restores a previously exported configuration and regenerates the universe. Custom Primitives, Superposition layers and supported presentation state belong to the configuration workflow.

EXPLORE · OBSERVER

NEAR ↔ FAR changes your view through the completed generated universe. Natural Zoom provides the same exploration more directly through the pointer or touch.

TRAVEL · GO vs ENGAGE

  • GO — travel normally toward the deepest, primordial part of the generated universe.
  • ENGAGE — enter WARP and continue into the infinite mathematical sequence.
WARP EXPERIENCE
When WARP begins, the Bridge disappears so the universe fills the screen. Press G to bring it back. If accumulated layers exist, ENGAGE warns before entering WARP because the current superposition may be discarded.

HELP & SHORTCUTS

Ctrl/Cmd+S — export the current configuration.
Ctrl/Cmd+Z — undo the last generation; in the Primitive Editor, undo the last point.
H — open this manual.
G — show/hide the Bridge controls; in WARP, the first press restores the Bridge.

RESET

RESET restores the Genesis welcome configuration and regenerates it again. Your persisted Custom Primitive remains part of the browser's local state and is not replaced by SURPRISE ME.

The 0.8 idea
Genesis 0.8 is not about giving Genesis more power. It is about making the encounter better: welcome → inhabit → look closer → be surprised.
